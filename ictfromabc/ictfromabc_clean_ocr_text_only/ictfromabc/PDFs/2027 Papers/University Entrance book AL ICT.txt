A Z-score is a number that tells us how well a student performed in a subject compared to
other students who sat for the same A/L examination. It helps to rank students fairly when

How you can increase your Z-score.

The Z-score is a measure of how well a student performs in comparison to others. There are
two main ways to increase your Z-score:
Z-ලකුණ යනු අනෙක් සිසුන්ට සාපේක්ෂව ශිෂ්‍යයෙකු කෙතරම් හොඳින් වැඩ කරනවාද යන්න පිලිබද
මැනීමකි. ඔබේ Z-ලකුණ වැඩි කරගැනීමට ප්‍රධාන ක්‍රම දෙකක් පවතී:

If the overall performance of other students is low
අනෙකුත් සිසුන්ගේ සමස්ත කාර්ය සාධනය අඩු නම්

Since the Z-score depends on the average mark and standard deviation, if most students score
lower, the average decreases. This makes your score relatively higher, increasing your Z-score.
However, this is not something you can control.
