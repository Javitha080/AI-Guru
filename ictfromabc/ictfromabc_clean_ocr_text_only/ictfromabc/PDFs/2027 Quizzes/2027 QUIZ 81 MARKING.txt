A PET scanner stands for Positron Emission Tomography scanner. It is a medical imaging device used
primarily in the field of nuclear medicine.
PET ස්කෑනරය යන්නෙන් Positron Emission Tomography ස්කෑනරය අදහස් කරයි. එය මූලික වශයෙන්

It works by detecting gamma rays emitted by a radioactive tracer, which is typically introduced into the
body via injection. These tracers are often molecules tagged with a radioactive isotope that undergoes
decay, emitting positrons.
