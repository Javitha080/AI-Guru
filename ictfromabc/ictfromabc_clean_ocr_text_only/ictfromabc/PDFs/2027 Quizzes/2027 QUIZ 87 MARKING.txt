Explanation / පැහැදිලි කිරීම

Modern computers primarily use the binary number system because it aligns perfectly with the way digital
electronic circuitry operates. Binary consists of only two digits, 0 and 1, which correspond to the two states of
electronic components: "off" (low voltage) and "on" (high voltage). This simplicity allows for the creation of
reliable, efficient, and cost-effective hardware, as circuits can be designed to detect and process just two
distinct voltage levels. Unlike decimal or other systems, binary minimizes the complexity of signal
representation and transmission in electronic devices.
