An EEG is a medical device used to measure the electrical activity of the brain. It records the brain's
electrical signals through small electrodes attached to the scalp. EEGs are commonly used to diagnose
and monitor conditions such as epilepsy, sleep disorders, and other neurological conditions by analyzing
brain wave patterns. Unlike imaging techniques like MRI, PET or CAT, which show brain structure or
blood flow, the EEG focuses specifically on real-time brain electrical activity.
