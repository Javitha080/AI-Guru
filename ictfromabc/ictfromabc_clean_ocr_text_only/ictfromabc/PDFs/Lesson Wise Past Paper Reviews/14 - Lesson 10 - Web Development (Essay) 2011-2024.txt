Paragraph Tag (<p>):

A paragraph tag creates a block-level element. This means that the content within a <p> tag will typically
start on a new line, and there will be some vertical space (margin) above and below it, separating it from
surrounding content. Browsers usually render text within a paragraph tag with a default font size and line
height.

Paragraph tag එකක්‌ බිලොක්‌ මටිටමේ මුලාංගයක්‌ නිරමාණය කරයි. මෙයින්‌ අදහස්‌ කරන්නේ <p> ටැගයක්‌
