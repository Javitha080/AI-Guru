How is z score calculated?
Z score tells you how far your marks are above or below the average mark of a subject.
It’s Your position relative to everyone else.

                               •x = Your mark
                               •μ (mu) = Average mark of all students in that subject
                               •σ (sigma) = Standard deviation (how spread-out marks are)
          (x−μ)
       Z=                              Small σ → students got similar marks
            σ                          Large σ → marks are very different
                                       It’s the difficulty adjustment factor

Paper was easy → Z-scores compress.
Paper was hard → moderate marks can give high Z-score
Everyone scored high → your advantage reduces.
